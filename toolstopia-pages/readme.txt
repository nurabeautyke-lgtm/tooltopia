TOOLTOPIA STORE PAGES (plugin, v1.9.0)  -  RECOMMENDED

WHAT IT DOES
- Creates or updates all your information pages with clean, compliant, designed content:
  About Us, Shipping Policy, Returns & Refund Policy, Delivery & Installation,
  Contact Us, FAQ, Why Choose Tooltopia Store, Brands We Carry, Wholesale & Bulk Orders,
  Request a Quote, Payment Methods, Today's Deals.
- Content is written to avoid unsupported marketing claims (no "100% genuine",
  "authorised distributor", "warranty-backed" guarantees, or invented statistics),
  which is important for Google policy and manual reviews.
- The Contact page now includes business hours and an embedded map.
- Does NOT change your homepage, theme, or design in any way.

HOW TO USE (about 2 minutes, nothing technical)
1. In WordPress: Plugins > Add New > Upload Plugin > choose this zip > Install Now.
   If a previous version is installed, deactivate and delete it first, then upload this one.
2. Activate the plugin. A green notice appears listing every page it set up,
   each with a "view page" link. Click them to confirm the new content is live.
3. To push updated content again later, bump the Version number in the plugin header
   and the TT_PAGES_VERSION constant, then reload any admin page. The plugin re-syncs
   all pages to the content in this file.

IMPORTANT NOTE ON EDITS
- On each new version, this plugin overwrites the title and content of the pages it
  manages with the content defined in the file. If you want to keep manual edits to a
  managed page, make those edits here in the plugin (or do not bump the version).

WHY A PLUGIN
It keeps your existing homepage and design untouched and avoids theme-activation issues.
This is the safest, simplest way to keep the information pages consistent.
