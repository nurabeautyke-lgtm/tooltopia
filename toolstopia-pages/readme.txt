TOOLS TOPIA PAGES (plugin, v1.0.0)  -  RECOMMENDED

WHAT IT DOES
- Creates or updates all your information pages with detailed, designed content:
  About Us, Shipping Policy, Returns & Refund Policy, Delivery & Installation,
  Contact Us, FAQ, Why Choose Tools Topia.
- Removes the word "card" from the product-page payment line.
- Does NOT change your homepage, theme, or design in any way.
- Everything stays editable in Pages afterwards.

HOW TO USE (2 minutes, nothing technical)
1. In WordPress: Plugins > Add New > Upload Plugin > choose this zip > Install Now > Activate.
2. A green notice appears listing every page it set up, each with a "view page" link.
   Click them to confirm the new content is live.
3. Edit any page later in Pages - your edits are kept and will not be overwritten.

WHY A PLUGIN
It keeps your existing premium homepage and design untouched, and avoids any theme-activation
issues. This is the safest, simplest option.
